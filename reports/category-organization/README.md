# Trays, Covers and Storage review

Reviewed all 42 product photographs and names against their purpose.

- Trays: 7 bench, dividing and diamond trays used for sorting and handling parts.
- Covers: 4 standalone dust covers or lids.
- Storage: 31 boxes, tins, container sets and covered organisers.

Moved RIT-0237, RIT-0243 and RIT-0244 from Trays to Storage. Although their names include “tray”, their photographs and descriptions show removable round containers in covered organisers. Grouping them with other container sets makes the storage range consistent.

Kept RIT-0025 (Plastic Dividing Tray) in Trays because its primary function is sorting parts in fixed tray compartments.

Checked products outside these groups with tray, cover, storage, container, tin or box in their names. RIT-0102 and RIT-0103 are clock covers and stay in Clock Parts and Accessories; RIT-0154 is a soldering tray and stays in Soldering and Helping Hands. RIT-0116 is a clock key set and RIT-0221 is a screwdriver set; their boxes do not change their primary category.

Website data and generated pages were updated. The downloadable PDF and Excel exports were not regenerated as part of this category review.
